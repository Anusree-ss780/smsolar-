Questions we hear most

Will solar work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes, at lower output. Grid-tied systems draw from the grid when needed and send extra back when the sun is strong.

How much subsidy can I get?

Homes can get central subsidy of up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more. We file the application for you. Rules can change, so we confirm in your quote.

What happens during a power cut?

A standard grid-tied system switches off for safety. Add a battery and your essentials keep running.

How often do panels need cleaning?

A rinse every 2–4 weeks is enough. We include 5 years of free service visits.
